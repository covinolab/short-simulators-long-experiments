# Horizon audit — consolidated findings and insight record

Hand-written companion to the auto-generated `report.md` in this directory. That report is
regenerated by every `--phase analyze`; THIS document is the durable record of what was learned,
in what order, and why several first readings had to be corrected — so the insights are not lost
and the dead ends are not re-walked. Methods live in
`Script_Bank/Analysis/SRM_AND_SBI_DIMER_ALP_Horizon_Audit.md`; every number below is traceable to
an artifact named in §10.

## 1 · The question and the design

The estimator trains on independently initialized 2 s simulations but is deployed on consecutive
2 s windows of continuous 20 s recordings; later windows inherit state (populations, spatial
organization, photophysics) the training distribution never contains. The audit measures what that
costs, under the simulator itself, where truth is knowable: per theta, ONE continuous 20 s
recording sliced into ten windows (deployment condition) versus TEN independently initialized,
independently seeded 2 s clips (training condition = "ideal"), identical theta and imaging,
the trained production estimator (`Estimator.npz`, sha1 `6339f60a359e` = the Epoch-50 −7.48
snapshot) reading every window. Estimate = per-window posterior summary from 1,000 unrestricted
flow draws (marginal median for rates/speeds; composition formed inside each draw). Primary
estimands predeclared (f_D, D_A, kappa_OFF); three-outcome verdicts against prespecified margins
(= the in-model error of the 10,000-video recovery); the trajectory is the replicate; all CIs are
trajectory bootstraps. Cohort `936727a9bd6b` (lineage `0fd0220c2a38`), T = 502 drawn from the training prior (master seed
20260825, per-(theta, arm, replicate) seeds via SeedSequence), R = 10; **500/500 completed** (cohort extended to 502 draws; theta 106 and 281 retained but unrealizable — see 3b).

## 1a · Where the composition truth comes from (made explicit 2026-08-26)

Worth stating plainly, because the manuscript previously left it implicit and a reader could have
guessed wrong. The composition truth is **observed inside the simulator, not reconstructed**:

* `species_counts_per_frame` counts, for every recorded frame, the particles whose coordinates are
  finite in each species channel of the dense pose tensor -> an exact (A, B, C) trace.
* That is the SAME tensor that is then collapsed over species and handed to `render_dli_video`,
  so the truth and the video the estimator sees come from one physical realization. The runner
  asserts the trajectory's frame count equals the declared frame count before either is used.
* Window j's primary truth is the population at its FIRST frame, which is what training labelled
  (training labels are each clip's initial counts). Mean and end are kept as sensitivity
  references only.
* The estimator's count coordinates are converted to f_D INSIDE each posterior draw and compared
  against that trajectory-derived f_D.

Two consequences worth keeping straight. First, the state-support escape (52.5% of late windows
outside the trained count box) is a statement about the SIMULATOR'S OWN states, not about
estimates: a parameter drawn inside the prior does not stay inside it as a state, so a box prior
over initial per-species counts is not closed under the model's dynamics. Second, only f_D is a
confirmatory state claim here. The per-species counts are audited against the stale t=0 label and
therefore read state DRIFT, not estimator error; direct per-species comparison against the
window-local ReaDDy counts is possible with the data already on disk but has not been run, and no
such claim is made.

## 2 · Corrections that shaped the result (do not re-learn these)

1. **State truth = the WINDOW-START population** (the estimator's training labels are the initial
   counts of each training clip). Judging the composition against the within-window mean
   manufactured a ~27 pp fake error where the true start-referenced error is 2.9 pp (9.2x
   inflation). Mean/end/unrounded-label are sensitivity references only.
2. **Primary statistic = paired ABSOLUTE-error contrast**; the signed contrast is the bias read.
   At window 10, kappa_OFF errors split 60% high / 40% low (over-estimates average x3.25,
   under-estimates x0.37): accuracy reads ±202% while the direction reads +37% — both are needed,
   and magnitude-only reporting makes bias look like noise.
3. **Coverage is never pooled across estimand kinds** (pooled, it faked a decline: constants 100%
   vs stale-truth counts 0% at w9).
4. **Seeds spawned per (theta, arm, replicate)** from a persisted master seed; resets are
   independently randomized. ReaDDy's internal dynamics RNG is not exposed (documented).
5. **Cohort identity digests** stamped in every artifact and verified before use; `prepare`
   refuses a dirty cohort dir. `--phase selftest` covers all six decision-critical logic families.
6. **MAP vs marginal median: interchangeable.** 10K recovery set with truth: overall MAE 0.1678
   vs 0.1682 dex; swapping changes recovery MAE ≤0.4% in every stratum; on the 500 real windows
   |MAP−median| is flat across window positions (kappa_OFF 0.016→0.018 dex) — two orders below
   the audit's effects. Under MAP the kappa_OFF headline would read ≈+31% instead of +37%
   (median sits 4.8% above MAP on recovery). The median-vs-SGM −26% flag from n=6 smoke windows
   is attributed to SGM snap-to-one-draw discreteness in a wide 10-D posterior; SGM-at-scale on
   the reset arm (the ONLY dataset holding joint draws AND truth) remains a consistency check.
   No GPU is needed to COMPUTE an SGM — only to generate draws.
7. **Units and direction, always**: plain % (±50% = off by half; ±200% = off by x3) and pp
   (receptors per 100), one stated truth reference, direction column mandatory.

## 2b · Two corrections from external review (2026-08-26)

**1. f_D is the DIMER-COMPLEX fraction, not a receptor fraction.** The kernel's `DIMER_INDEX`
selects `dimer_complex_fraction` = (B+C)/(A+B+C): of all molecular complexes present, the share
that are dimers. The manuscript had described those same values as "of 100 receptors, how many
are bound in dimers", which is the SEPARATE quantity `receptors_in_dimers`
f_R = 2(B+C)/(A+2B+2C) that the kernel computes at another index. The two differ substantially:
the census is 31.4% / 78.2% in f_D and 46.4% / 86.2% in f_R. **Every computed number was
correct; only the manuscript's words were wrong**, and the census report and progress summary had
labelled both quantities correctly all along. Terminology fixed everywhere: say "dimer-complex
fraction", never "paired fraction", which invites exactly this confusion.

**2. The verdict rule tested zero before the margin, so the outcomes overlapped.** A CI could lie
entirely above zero AND entirely inside the margin; whichever test ran first won, and zero ran
first, so D_A was reported "degraded" while being smaller than the error the estimator already
carries. The margin is what was predeclared to matter, so the margin now decides: `degraded`
needs the whole CI above the margin, `equivalent` the whole CI inside it. Statistical
detectability is reported in its own column, because with 500 settings almost any nonzero effect
is detectable. The headline is now clean and unchanged in substance: **f_D degraded, kappa_OFF
degraded, D_A practically equivalent (detectable, and below its margin)**.

Also corrected in the write-up: "state-mediated" overclaimed causal mediation and became
"associated with inherited state rather than elapsed time" (the state variables leave ~89% of
window-level variance unexplained); photophysical memory is "disfavored", not "contradicted",
since the experiment never resets photophysics independently; large log errors are reported as
multiplicative factors (x3.02) rather than +-202%, which is wrong on a log scale; and the
experimental bias transfer is presented as a BOUND, never a correction ("the in-simulator trend
is one fifth the magnitude of the experimental rise", not "explains one fifth of it").

### 3a · Stratified margins (T = 500; part of `--phase analyze`)

Motivation (user challenge, correct): recovery error is heteroscedastic in the true value, so a
prior-averaged margin can mislead. Confirmed on the margin side with counts (median-based, pooled
A/B/C): recovery MAE is ±68% at 1–7 emitters, ±38% at 7–46, ±9% at 46–316 — the pooled count
margin is dominated by the sparse end, one more reason counts stay exploratory-only.

Design (defensibility): strata = fixed thirds of the PRIOR RANGE in log10 (prespecified geometry,
not post-hoc quantiles; the prior is uniform in log10, so thirds carry equal expected mass; audit
cohort, reset arm, and the 10K recovery set all draw from the same prior). Per-stratum margin =
recovery MAE inside the stratum, from marginal medians. Statistic = the SAME headline w10 paired
added error, bootstrapped over the trajectories WITHIN the stratum. f_D uses prespecified
composition bins and the SHARED composition kernel (so the audit and the census compose f_D
identically); its per-stratum margins are point-composed from recovery medians (prior-averaged
2.81 pp) while the audit's f_D is composed inside each draw (declared margin 2.8 pp) — the offset
is stated in the report, never silently mixed.

### 3b · The two unrealizable draws, and how the cohort was topped up

**What failed.** Theta **106** (A=206, B=207, C=185; 598 emitters) and **281** (A=69, B=311, C=7;
386 emitters) never produced a continuous 20 s render.

**Density is NOT the explanation** (checked, 2026-08-26): three completed draws are DENSER than
theta 106, the densest at 747 emitters, and 30 completed draws are denser than theta 281. What
distinguishes theta 106 is that it is almost perfectly balanced across A/B/C, i.e. maximal
reaction traffic, and render memory scales with particle IDENTITIES accumulated over the render —
a 20 s continuous render of a high-traffic draw keeps creating them. Renderability is therefore a
property of the draw, but of its dynamics, not of its initial count.

**Retry attempted and abandoned.** rcl01 idle, 60 GB free, strictly sequential (the documented fix
for the original concurrent-run failure). Theta 106's ReaDDy simulation finished in 9.6 s; the
render then reached 60 GB RAM plus 66 GB swap (>128 GB demand, still climbing), process in
uninterruptible sleep, ~230 MB/s swap traffic against ~97% idle-wait — thrashing, not progress.
Killed; memory recovered fully; no partial artifacts (verified 498/498, digest intact). An earlier
"~35 GB peak" estimate was WRONG by more than 3x: the planning figure for a high-traffic 20 s
render is **>128 GB**, which rules out rcl01 (62 GB) and would need a big-memory node.

**Resolution: extend, do not substitute in place.** A correction to an argument recorded here
earlier, which claimed that adding fresh draws would bias the cohort by replacing extremes with
typical draws. That reasoning was WRONG, and the user was right to reject it: the completed draws
are ALREADY a sample of the prior conditioned on being realizable — the failures are by definition
absent from it — so drawing further theta from the same uniform prior and keeping those that
complete samples that SAME conditional distribution. This is ordinary rejection sampling; it adds
draws without moving the distribution, and only reduces variance. The conditioning happened when
those two failed, not when replacements were added.

Mechanized as `--phase extend --n-extra N` (kernel-side lineage support, selftest family 8):
fresh draws are appended from a stream seeded by (master seed, generation), the cohort digest is
recomputed, and the previous id is pushed onto a `lineage` list so every completed artifact stays
valid — its theta row is untouched and is still checked exactly, so lineage membership alone can
never admit a mismatched artifact. The unrealizable draws are RETAINED in the cohort file on
purpose: deleting them would erase the record of what could not be simulated. Applied here:
500 -> 502 theta, cohort `936727a9bd6b` (lineage `0fd0220c2a38`), new indices 500 (64 emitters)
and 501 (57 emitters), both ordinary draws around the 27th density percentile.

**Outcome.** Theta 500 and 501 generated in ~50 s each with memory untouched (60 GB free
throughout — nothing like the >128 GB thrash), inferred and re-analyzed: the audit now rests on
**500 completed trajectories**, cohort `936727a9bd6b`. Every one of the 498 earlier artifacts was
accepted through the lineage check without being rewritten. The headline numbers barely moved
(f_D +5.395 -> +5.392 pp; D_A +0.008 -> +0.007; kappa_OFF +0.295 -> +0.295 dex) and no verdict
changed, which is the expected result of adding 0.4% more draws — this was a completeness
question, not a statistical-power one.

## 3c · The recording-level audit (added 2026-08-26, external review)

The per-window numbers are a LOCAL audit. The experimental workflow does not report a window: it
averages a recording's ten windows into ONE value per cell, and the cell is the biological
replicate. Applying that same production operator to each arm answers the operationally relevant
question at no extra simulation cost.

| quantity | reset recording error | continuous recording error | added | margin | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| f_D | 1.71 pp | 5.56 pp | +3.86 [3.38, 4.37] pp | 2.8 pp | degraded |
| D_A | x1.030 (0.013 dex) | x1.045 (0.019 dex) | +0.006 [0.003, 0.009] dex | x1.05 | **equivalent** |
| kappa_OFF | x1.38 (0.139 dex) | x2.22 (0.346 dex) | +0.206 [0.189, 0.224] dex | x1.50 | degraded |

**Aggregation helps but does not rescue.** Every error falls relative to the single-window figures
(f_D 8.3 -> 5.6 pp, kappa_OFF x3.02 -> x2.22, D_A x1.07 -> x1.045) because averaging cancels the
zero-mean part of the error. No verdict changes, because averaging preserves a bias. What survives
aggregation is therefore systematic, which is the whole point: a quantity whose windows degrade
only noisily would have been rescued here, and none of these are.

**The consequence per estimand, which is the paper's real message.** D_A: the workflow is
validated, at both levels. f_D: individual late windows are not nominally calibrated, but the
recording-level condition contrast is extremely robust -- the artifact moves 46.8 pp by ~1.2 pp --
so the comparative biology stands while absolute compositions carry a stated few-pp artifact.
kappa_OFF: a single time-aggregated value should NOT be read as the physical constant, because
compression makes the recording summary depend on which states that trajectory happened to visit
rather than on one underlying rate. This is a SELECTIVE verdict, not a repudiation of windowing:
window 1 is exchangeable with the reset arm, so slicing is sound in itself.

## 5 · The legacy question (old kappa_OFF "stable at ~1/s"): facts vs interpretation

  absorbed the drift (0.254 → 0.320 µm²/s across the recording, x1.26, 2–3x ABOVE the published

  Current pipeline: D_A pinned flat at 0.124/0.133 (inside published) and the non-stationarity
  surfaces in kappa_OFF. Supporting evidence: the recordings demonstrably change along their
  length (bleaching at minimum), so a window-wise fit must express the change somewhere; the old
  fit's one externally checkable parameter (D_A) was wrong AND drifting; and the old stability
  was condition-specific — FAB kappa_OFF drifted x1.54 — which a genuinely robust kappa_OFF
  readout should not show. External anchor — CORRECTED (user, 2026-08-25): quantitative
  published data for the dissociation scale DOES exist — apparent complex lifetimes of
  1.13 ± 0.06 s and 0.80 ± 0.03 s (Li 2026; recorded in progress_summary §2 and table A3),
  inverting to a ~0.9–1.3 /s scale. Caveats stand (apparent lifetimes are termination-limited,
  so the inverted scale tends to OVERSTATE the true rate; per-complex quantity from a different
  assay, not the model's B→A+A constant), but the anchor is real. Old flat INLB (~1.56 /s) sits
  ~1.2–1.7x above it; the new pipeline sits further above (1.75 first window, 2.8–3.5
  aggregated). The anchor therefore weighs FOR the genuine-stability reading. The ledger's "no
  dissociation rate is published" is citation discipline for the paper (never cite a rate
  constant to Li), not a reason to discount the anchor internally. (That 1/s is also the prior
  center remains a residual coincidence, no longer a main argument.) Old-vs-new normalization was checked and ruled out as the
  differentiator (both are fixed global dtype-max scaling).
- **What keeps the alternative alive (user challenge 2026-08-25, correct to raise):** the NEW
  estimator has a PROVEN +37% late-window kappa_OFF inflation (§3); the OLD estimator was never
  horizon-audited and could be less horizon-sensitive; and only 2 of the old array's 10
  parameters were examined — the drift could also sit in unchecked ones. Discriminating tests
  (approval-gated, §9 item 8): (a) all-10-parameter window-trend + per-cell kappa_OFF dispersion

  stability; (b) decisive: run the audit's continuous/reset synthetic windows (constant true
  kappa_OFF) through the old estimator — flat-and-accurate = robustness, flat-but-pinned =
  insensitivity.

## 6 · Mechanism: why kappa_OFF RISES within a perfect simulator

Within-trajectory decomposition (494×10 windows, demeaned per trajectory so every theta-level
confound cancels; approved 2026-08-25):

- Raw time trend r = +0.105; after controlling the measured STATE variables (start f_D, net
  within-window f_D change, |change|, flow leakage) the residual time trend is r = −0.05:
  **elapsed time per se has no leftover effect — the rise is associated with inherited state** (an association; causal mediation is not established, joint R² ≈ 0.11). This argues
  against accumulated photophysics (a pure function of elapsed time) as the in-simulator driver.
- Binned in plain units, the over-read is an **inverted U in composition**: +10% when the window
  starts monomer-dominated (f_D<0.2), **+80–85% in mixed windows (f_D 0.2–0.8)**, +19% when
  dimer-dominated — largest exactly where reaction traffic (both species abundant) is maximal.
- Joint R² = 0.107: these coarse state summaries explain ~11% of window-to-window error variance;
  the rest is per-window estimation noise. Joint betas are suppression-inflated (start-f_D and
  net-change are collinear); the univariate and binned reads are the trustworthy ones.
- Candidate ranking (direction-pattern evidence, not yet a controlled result):
  **spatial memory / geminate re-binding favored** — dissociation leaves correlated A pairs that
  re-associate far faster than uniform mixing, inflating visible event rates; predicts f_D OVER
  (observed +4.3 pp), kappa_OFF OVER, kappa_IMM over, D_A slightly under — all observed.
  Photophysical memory (bleached-fluorophore dimers masquerading as monomers) predicts f_D UNDER
  — contradicted — and a residual time trend — also contradicted.
- The controlled separations are designed and documented in the companion: (a) photophysics-reset
  render of continuous windows; (b) boundary-state-matched reset arm (fresh uniform placement at
  each window's true counts — kills spatial correlations, keeps composition). Both need rcl01.

## 7 · Conclusions

All terms used here are defined above: the two arms and the window index in §1, the units and the
truth reference in §2 (corrections 1 and 7), the margins and verdicts in §3.

1. **The reset-training assumption fails measurably under the model's own dynamics.** From
   window 1 to window 10 the f_D error triples (±2.9 → ±8.3 pp) and the kappa_OFF error roughly
   quadruples (±53% → ±202%); both breach their prespecified margins. D_A moves only
   ±5% → ±7% and passes. Window-1 exchangeability passed, so the damage is inherited state, not
   arm construction — and it persists inside the trained box (§3), so it is not mere
   extrapolation.
2. **The failure is a one-directional bias with unshaken confidence.** Late windows are read as a
   more chemically active scene than they are — f_D +4.3 pp OVER, kappa_OFF +37% OVER — while the
   90% posterior intervals cover the truth only 39% of the time at window 10.
3. **The driver is inherited state, not elapsed time** (§6): the time trend vanishes under state
   controls; the over-read is an inverted U in composition, peaking at +80–85% in mixed windows.
   The direction pattern favors geminate re-binding and contradicts photophysical memory; the
   controlled separations remain to be run (§9).
4. **The model's own dynamics leave the training prior** (§3): 52.5% of late windows have true
   counts outside the trained box (extinctions and the A ceiling) with receptors exactly
   conserved. The count prior needs a floor fix as much as a ceiling fix.
5. **The biology results survive with quantified corrections, none withdrawn** (§4): the
   condition contrast is safe (understated by ~1.2 pp); absolute censuses read ~2–3 pp high; the
   within-recording decline is strengthened; the experimental kappa_OFF rise is only ~22%
   mechanically explained; D_A claims untouched.
6. **The legacy flat kappa_OFF is verified as real; its meaning is UNDECIDED with evidence on
   BOTH sides** (§5): displaced misfit is supported by the old pipeline's D_A (wrong and
   drifting, 2–3× published) and its non-flat FAB kappa_OFF; genuine stability is supported by
   the published-lifetime anchor (~0.9–1.3 /s), which the old value (~1.56) matches more closely
   than the new pipeline's (1.75–3.5). The old estimator was never horizon-audited. Two
   discriminating tests are specified in §5 and §9; the §3a compression finding (late windows
   pull kappa_OFF toward the prior interior) supplies the mechanism that WOULD produce a flat
   ~1 /s profile from an insensitive readout, strengthening that side without settling it.
7. **The margins survive stratification** (§3a): with per-stratum margins from the same recovery
   set, kappa_OFF stays degraded in the slow and fast terciles (2.2x / 1.7x the local margins)
   and f_D in every usable bin; D_A is robust everywhere. The horizon-added shift is a
   monotone compression toward the prior interior (+243% / +77% / −51% across kappa_OFF
   terciles) plus a genuine over-read visible net-of-compression in the middle tercile.

## 7b · A reproducibility caveat worth knowing

Point estimates in `report.md` are exactly reproducible; the **bootstrap CIs are not
bit-reproducible across code versions**. The analyze phase draws from one seeded generator, so
adding a new resampling step (the stratified table, the in-box control) changes how much of that
stream earlier steps consume and the intervals shift in the third decimal — e.g. the in-box
paired delta read [+3.97, +6.97] before the stratified block was added and [+4.04, +7.06] after,
with the point estimate unmoved at +5.46. This is bootstrap noise, not instability in the result,
but it means a quoted interval should be refreshed from the current `report.md` rather than from
an older transcript. Fixing it properly would mean giving each resampling step its own child
generator; noted, not done.

## 8 · Operational lessons (cost real time; do not repeat)

- **Render memory scales with particle IDENTITIES over the trajectory** (each dissociation mints
  new IDs), not simultaneous occupancy: ~25–35 GB per dense 20 s theta. 16 workers swapped a
  62 GB box; 10 still swapped; **6 was safe for typical theta, 1-at-a-time for the densest, and
  two theta fit nowhere on rcl01**. Generation cost is dominated ~94% by `render_dli_video`
  (single-threaded numpy); ReaDDy is ~5% and its n_threads knob is NOT needed —
  the canonical fix is the `del smut; gc.collect()` teardown (in `simulation_rds_runner.py` and
  now in `horizon_audit_runner.py`), without which each ReaDDy run leaks ~50–120 threads.
- Per-theta generation wall time spans **79 s – 833 s** (10x, emitter-count-driven): budget from
  measured distributions, not single-point estimates.
- T=500 ran chunked (generate 100 → infer → strip videos) to keep peak disk ~5 GB; stripped theta
  files keep counts/seeds/stamps but need regeneration for re-inference.
- Statistical sizing: each theta contributes ONE number to the primary test (windows are
  correlated); equivalence costs ~10x more theta than detection (n ≈ 6.19·σ²/margin²).
